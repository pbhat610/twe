System architecture · Nov 2026

+twe AI Interviewer

Two browsers, one Next.js app, a handful of outside services. The recruiter signs in and works by interview id; the candidate never signs in and works by a secret invite token. Every outside service sits behind a port, and container.ts picks the adapter from env.
